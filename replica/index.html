About Getaway

Getaway is a low-poly 3D police chase in the style of Escape Road. Your car drives itself forward, and your job is to steer it through an endless city, grab coins and power-ups, and stay ahead of the police for as long as you can. The longer you survive, the higher your wanted level climbs and the more patrol cars join the chase.

A run ends when your car is wrecked (health hits zero) or you get busted (stopped with two police cars pinning you in). Every run is saved to your current save slot automatically.

How to play

  • Steer: left and right arrow keys, or A and D. On touch screens, hold the left or right half of the game.
  • Brake and reverse: down arrow or S, or hold both sides of the screen. Handy for tight corners and getting unstuck.
  • Pause: P or the space bar. The game also pauses while the admin panel is open.
  • Score: you score points for distance driven. Coins you pick up go straight into your save.
  • Watch the traffic: civilian cars drive along the roads. Hitting them damages your car, and wrecked cars stay where they stop.
  • Don't get boxed in: if two police cars trap you while you're stopped, you have about two seconds to get moving before you're busted.

Vehicles

Buy vehicles with coins in the Garage tab of the Saves panel, then pick one from the Driving menu. Flying vehicles pass over buildings; heavy vehicles knock police cars into a spin when you ram them.

Power-ups

Glowing boxes appear on the road as you drive. Drive through one to use it straight away. Upgrade them in the Garage to make them last longer and hit harder.

  • Health Pack: restores health.
  • Shield: no damage for a few seconds, and police cars that hit you spin out.
  • Nitro Boost: a burst of extra top speed.
  • Oil Slick: nearby police cars spin out.
  • Smoke Screen: the police lose track of you for a while.
  • EMP Burst: knocks out nearby police cars.
  • Repair Kit: repairs your car over a few seconds.
  • Mega Magnet: pulls in every coin nearby.

Areas

The further you drive from the start, the more the city changes: City, Suburbs, Desert, Ocean/Beach, Snowy Mountains, Forest and finally the Industrial Zone. Reaching a new area ticks it off in the Unlocks tab.

Saves and cheats

Open Saves to manage up to three save slots. Each slot keeps its own health, coins, level, vehicles, item upgrades and run history. Damage carries over between runs, so repair your car in the Garage when it gets low. If you start a run with a wrecked car, it's repaired automatically for 100 coins, or patched up for free if you're broke.

The admin panel (🔒 Admin in the Saves panel, or Ctrl+Shift+A) changes your save directly, so cheats work in the game straight away, even in the middle of a run: god mode, health, coins, level, wanted level, vehicles and item upgrades.

FAQ

Where are my saves stored?

In this browser only. Use Backup in the Saves panel to copy a save code or download a file, then import it on another device.

Can I import saves from the Escape Road 3 page?

Yes. Save codes and files from the original page use the same format, so they import here unchanged.

The police keep catching me. Any tips?

Take corners early, use the brake before tight turns, and save Smoke Screen and EMP for when you're surrounded. Faster vehicles like the Interceptor or F1 Racing Car make a big difference at high wanted levels.
